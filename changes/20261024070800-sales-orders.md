- Manual sales orders at Sales → Sales orders. A draft has no number until it is approved. The number is an integer shown as SO27766, seeded from 27766. Channel is a lookup separate from how the order arrived. Payment method, payment date, and a payment reference are stored on the order, along with nullable eBay fees and earnings. A product line must be allocated to SKUs before approval; freight, fees, and the other line kinds do not. Prices, including the eBay amounts, stay off the page for roles that cannot view financials.
