- A shipped, packed, or packing sales order can change the order reference, contact, terms, shipping, addresses, notes, and files. Line quantity and SKU stay locked.
- Bill-to and ship-to each have a Company field. Ship-to company is separate from bill-to.
- An email order fills a blank contact from the sender when the customer has no default contact.
- Payment terms include Net 10, Net 14, Net 45, and Net 90.
