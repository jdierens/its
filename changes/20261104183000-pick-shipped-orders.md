- A shipped or partially shipped sales order can record a pick and a pack. Finish leaves that status, the ship date, and the shipments as they are.
- Warehouse → Picking lists shipped orders that still have no finished pick. Warehouse → Packing lists shipped orders whose pick is finished and whose pack is still empty.
- Cancelled, invoiced, and closed orders stay closed.
- On the sales order list, the stage icons open that order. Pick opens its pick sheet, pack opens its pack sheet, and invoice opens its invoice. Ship opens the order until there is a page for one shipment.
