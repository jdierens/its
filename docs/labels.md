# Item labels

**Labels** (`/labels`) prints the 90 mm × 29 mm item label. The layout, barcode, and QR template are described in `docs/items.md`. This page is the lookup.

One row holds **Label**, **Search**, **Qty on label**, **Copies**, and **Print**. Serial mode adds **Serial number** on that row. Each name sits above its field in the same caption style. The fields share one top and one height. **Print** is the same height as those fields and lines up with them. The search field shows about 25 characters. **Qty on label** and **Copies** are at least as wide as their captions, so the caption and the field share one column and the caption stays left-aligned. The numbers stay right-aligned, with the same horizontal padding as Search and extra room on the right for the caret. Enter runs the search. Typing searches on its own after a short pause. A barcode scanner that types and sends Enter uses the same field. A small clear icon inside the field empties it. There is no separate Search button. The search runs on the server through the service role, the same approved-user check as the rest of the item reads, and the response has no prices.

- The text is an exact SKU, ignoring case (`107132`) → that item is selected and the label is prepared. No extra confirmation.
- Other text (`SRM7C`) is a case-insensitive partial match on SKU, name, and description. `%`, `_`, and `\` in the text are escaped so they are not LIKE wildcards.
- One match on Enter selects that item. The page does not ask you to pick 1 of 1.
- Several matches show a short list under that row: SKU, name, and a one-line description. Tap or click a row, or press Enter on the highlighted row. That selects the item the same way an exact SKU does: the field shows the SKU (or the name when the SKU is empty), the label preview loads, and Print turns on. A blur of the search field does not clear that list before the row is chosen. If Print cannot run, the page says why.
- Nothing matches → **No items found**.
- Typing can search on its own, after a short pause. The field keeps what you type. A result is applied only when the field still shows the text that was searched, so a slower reply cannot replace a newer string. A SKU prefix does not select an item while you are still typing, even if only one row matches. An exact SKU does, once that pause lands on the exact SKU.
- The list stops at 25 rows and says to refine the search when more exist.

A link from the item page still opens `/labels?sku=` or `/labels?id=` and prepares that item. **Qty on label** is a whole number from 0 to 9999, right-aligned, and does not change stock. **Copies** is a whole number from 1 to 99, right-aligned, and starts at 1. A blank quantity becomes 0. A blank or zero copy count becomes 1. Both use the same typeface as Search, with tabular figures. **Print** is a printer icon. Its name and tooltip are Print. It stays off until an item is selected, and it matches the height of the fields beside it. **Label** sits at the start of the row, with the same caption above a two-part control: **Item** or **Serial**. On a wide screen every control shares one row. Below about 1180 px, Label and Search share the first line and Search fills the rest of it. Qty on label, Copies, and Print stay together on the next line. On a phone those controls are at least 44 px on the touch side, and Print lines up with the inputs.

## Serial label

**Serial** prints one label per serial for a box that is going out. The stock is DK-1202, a 62 × 100 mm die. The page is landscape, 100 mm wide by 62 mm tall: the 62 mm tape runs across the print head, so that side is the page height, and the 100 mm feed is the page width. The same Brother QL-820NWB is the printer. Paper size in the dialog is 62 × 100 mm, landscape, 100% scale, no margins, headers and footers off.

The first line is the SKU in bold, the description (up to two lines, then an ellipsis), and the quantity at the right edge, written `Qty 5`. The quantity is the same **Qty on label** field, and it is printed on every serial label in the batch. The second line is the serial as Code 128, with a ten-module quiet zone on each side, and the serial in text centered under the bars. The DDS mark is not printed. The words Serial and SKU are not printed.

**Serial number** is on that row on a wide screen, caption above. On a narrower window it is the full width of the line under Label and Search, and Qty on label, Copies, and Print stay together on the line after it. A scanner can type into it: autocapitalize, autocorrect, and spellcheck are off, and Enter adds the serial instead of running the search. Each accepted serial joins a short list under the row. Print makes one page per serial, then repeats that set for **Copies**. A serial still sitting in the field is included, so one serial does not need an extra Enter. Empty text is ignored. The same serial, ignoring case, is not added twice. The list stops at 30. Remove a serial with the button on its chip. Print stays off until an item is selected and at least one serial is present.

The serial is typed. Nothing checks it against stock. There is no serial table in this change.

The barcode row is a flex line. A later QR can sit on the left or the right of the bars without moving the human-readable serial, which stays under the bars. That QR is not printed. `serialLabelQrPayload` prepares `SKU|NAME|QTY|SERIAL` for it. NAME is the item name. QTY is the quantity number, from 0 to 9999, not the words `Qty 5`. Any `|` in a field is replaced with a space and the extra spaces are collapsed, so the text always splits into four parts.

`supabase/migrations/20261021210000_label_lookup_trgm.sql` adds trigram indexes on `items.name` and `items.description`. SKU already has one. The search works before that file is applied. It is not applied.
