# Sales orders

Phase 1 is the manual sales order: a draft, lines, SKU allocations, customer notes, a customer PO file, and approval. Fulfillment and invoice tables are created now so later phases do not add them. Capture from email and WhatsApp is not in this phase. An eBay pull can create a draft under the one eBay customer for an order dated 1 Jan 2026 or later. Jan reviews that draft and approves it. The pull does not assign `so_number` and does not approve the order. Orders dated before that stay out of the pull.

Sales orders do not share tables, counters, payment terms, or carrier checks with purchase orders. `so_counter` is separate from `po_counter`. Terms live in `so_payment_terms` (Net 7 is stored as `Net7` and sent to QuickBooks as `Net 7`). Payment In Advance is stored as `PIA` and sent to QuickBooks as `Payment In Advance`. Ship methods live in `so_ship_methods` (DHL WPX, FedEx IP, UPS Saver, and so on).

## Number

A draft has no number. `so_number` is an integer, and the order is shown as `SO` plus that integer, for example `SO27766`. `so_counter` is seeded at 27766, the next number at go-live.

Approval suggests the lowest unused number at or above the counter. The person approving can type a different number. `approve_sales_order` locks `so_counter`, rejects a number that is already on a sales order, and never moves the counter backwards. A number above the counter moves the counter past it. A number below the counter is allowed when it is free, and the counter stays where it is. Cancelling keeps a number that was already issued.

eBay orders use this same series. `ebay_order_id` is unique when set, and `ebay_sales_record_ref` stores the eBay sales record reference. A draft created from eBay still has no number. The sales-order list and the draft show the eBay order number and the sales record number. Approval assigns the SO number from `so_counter`, the same way as any other draft.

An eBay draft's product lines store the unit price and extension after the eBay discount. Buyer shipping is a freight line. `ebay_order_earnings` is eBay's total due the seller, not the merchandise total. `ebay_label_fee` is the shipping label bought on eBay, and seller net is earnings minus that fee. The next order pull refreshes the label fee when eBay has deducted one.

Before the first eBay pull, seed `ebay_order_id` and `ebay_sales_record_ref` from the legacy log map (Order Number and Sales Record No on the row whose REF is that SO number). The map stays out of git. Admin > Integrations > eBay accepts the CSV, updates matching sales orders, and does not create orders. Pull orders stays off until that seed row exists. Inside the 2026 window, an eBay order that already matches `ebay_order_id`, or a single `ebay_sales_record_ref`, updates that sales order instead of inserting another one. The customer on a new draft is the existing eBay Customer record. Buyer name and username are stored on the order. No per-buyer customer is created.

The form does not ask for an internal reference, and nothing requires one. `sales_orders.reference` stays in the database and is not shown on the list, the draft, or the order. A save of an order that already has one keeps that value. Order reference on the form is the customer PO. When the customer is eBay Customer, that same field is labeled eBay order id and shows `ebay_order_id`. On an eBay order, Buyer follows Customer on that row, and the eBay sales record number is the last field. Neither the internal reference nor the sales record number is a list column. The SO number itself is assigned at approval.

The form keeps that field order on the zinc surface (`ds` in `docs/design-system.md`). A sticky header holds the order identity, the sales order number, and the save, approve, and cancel actions as icons. The banner under it is row 1: channel, sales rep, customer, order date, and order reference. The field is titled Sales rep and still stores two initials. Its control is only wide enough for those letters, and the order date is tightened the same way. For the eBay customer the order reference is labeled eBay order id. An eBay order also shows Buyer after Customer and the eBay sales record number at the end of the row. An eBay order with empty payment terms or Incoterms starts as Payment In Advance (`PIA`) and FOB. Those defaults are on Admin > Settings. `supabase/migrations/20261025120000_ebay_default_terms.sql` adds the term and seeds the settings. Apply it by hand. Deploying does not run it. A value already saved on the draft stays. An eBay order leaves bill-to blank. Ship-to is not copied into bill-to on the pull, on open, or on save. The form does not show Bill to on an eBay order. Approval failures stay on the form as the database sentence, such as a line that is not allocated or a sales order number that is already used. Bill-to, ship-to, payment terms, ship method, sales rep, and order date are not required to approve. Terms and shipping are the next block, in a drawer that starts open: currency, payment terms, Incoterms, ship method, and carrier account. Ship-to sits above bill-to in the narrow column. Ship-to fields are line 1, line 2, city, state, postal code, country, then attention and phone. The line table columns are customer part number, customer description, customer qty, customer unit, and cust ext. Kind and condition stay off the screen. Under a product line, each allocated SKU is a table row in the same columns: the SKU name, allocated quantity under customer qty, and the line's unit and extension under customer unit and cust ext, then Remove. Find SKU sits under that row. Focusing it fills the customer part number when the field is empty, and matching items open without another click. The form does not show an Allocated n of n note. A pack label or an exact SKU is allocated when the line is empty. There is no Allocate button. eBay amounts follow the lines as one card: transaction fee, label fee, ad fee, and seller earnings in a row, with buyer total, seller earnings, shipping label, and seller net in those same columns underneath. The longer explanation stays behind About these amounts. Label fee and seller earnings keep a short info note. Then payment: payment date, payment method, payment reference, and payment amount. Below payment, one row holds customer notes, internal notes, and attachments. Uploads are customer PO PDFs. There is no file type control. Below 960px the columns stack in that same order. `payment_amount` is nullable cents on the sales order. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025083100_sales_order_payment_amount.sql` before relying on that amount. Deploying does not run it. Requested ship date stays stored and on the list, and is not a field on this form.

## Channel

`source` is how the order arrived: manual, email, WhatsApp, or eBay. `channel` is a different fact, a foreign key to `so_channels`: `ebay`, `broker_reseller`, `direct`, `supermicro_ff`, `oem_end_user`, `take_out`, `project_buy`, and `other`. Row level security is on, and only `service_role` can read or write that table.

`sheet_ref` is indexed and is not unique. `legacy_journal_ref` is nullable text and is not unique. The manual form does not fill either one.

## Invoices

`so_invoices.seq` is 1, 2, 3, and so on for that sales order. The display number is generated: seq 1 is `INV` plus the sales-order number (`INV27766`), and later invoices are `INV27766-2`, `INV27766-3`. The ITS invoice is the one the customer receives. `qbo_invoice_id`, `qbo_doc_number`, and `qbo_synced_at` are on the invoice for the later QuickBooks upload. An invoice cannot be inserted until the sales order has a number.

## Lines

`so_lines.line_kind` is `product`, `kit`, `freight`, `fee`, `service`, `discount`, or `other`. The default is `product`. The item is `so_line_allocations.item_id`, the same role as `po_lines.item_id`, and a product line can be several SKUs. Approval requires those quantities to add up to the line only when the kind is `product`. Freight, a fee, a service, a discount, a kit, and other do not need an item. Any allocation that is present still has to add up. Kit contents are not expanded. There is no link from a sales-order line to a purchase-order line.

## Approve

A product line needs allocations whose quantities add up to the line. A customer part that is a pack label (`106683_2PK`, `106683(x2)`, or `106683-2PK`) and is not itself an item needs the customer quantity times the pack. `106683_2PK` with customer quantity 1 allocates 2 of item `106683`. Several SKUs on one line are allowed, and a one-to-one line is still one allocation row. Approval requires a customer, at least one line, and a sales order number. Bill-to, ship-to, payment terms, ship method, carrier account, sales rep, and order date are not required. The check is in the database function. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025140000_approve_sales_order_optional_fields.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run it. That file keeps the pack quantity rule from `supabase/migrations/20261025100000_pack_allocation_qty.sql`.

Suggested SKUs are a prefix match after uppercasing and stripping spaces, `/`, and `-`. A customer part shorter than 3 characters does not match. A customer part that is an exact item SKU, or a pack label whose base SKU is exactly one item, is allocated when that line is still empty: on open, and when leaving Customer PN. There is no Allocate confirmation button. Find SKU stays on the line for a different item. It searches the same fields as the items page: SKU, name, description, model, part number, OEM name, OEM part number, product URL, attribute value, manufacturer, manufacturer alias, and eBay item id. It also searches the longest word in the customer part, splitting on spaces, punctuation, and underscores, so `FOO-BAR 106683_2PK` also looks up `106683`. Focusing the field fills the customer part number when it is empty, and the matches open on their own. Choosing one replaces that line's allocation. A pack that is not itself an item still allocates the piece quantity.

## Addresses and money

Ship-to is shown above bill-to. Choosing a customer copies both addresses, and they can be edited. An eBay order does not show Bill to, and saving it stores a blank bill-to. Ship-to stays on the order. Ship-to asks for attention and phone after the street, city, state, postal code, and country. **Save address to customer** writes them back with `updateContact`. A customer that already has a QuickBooks id is marked `pending`, and the existing customer push sends the change. If ship-to line 2 is blank, attention is copied into line 2. The ship-to phone updates the customer phone.

Choosing a customer also fills customer PO, channel, order date, requested ship date, terms, Incoterms, ship method, and carrier account from that customer's latest sales order. It does not copy an internal reference.

The list defaults to drafts that still need processing. **All orders** shows every status. Rows are newest order date first, and an order with no order date sorts last. The same date sorts by the time the row was created, newest first. Columns are number, customer, order reference, status, order date, ship date, and total. Order reference is the eBay order id, or the customer PO when the order is not from eBay. A draft with no SO number is labeled Draft.

On a line, quantity, unit price, and extension stay consistent. Changing the unit price recomputes the extension. Changing the extension, or the quantity, recomputes the unit price and keeps the extension. Unit price is four decimals. Extension is cents. The extension is editable. `write_sales_order` stores the extension as the cent rounding of quantity times unit price.

A customer part such as `106683_2PK` or `106683(x2)` is ITS SKU `106683` when that SKU is one item. The customer quantity, unit price, and extension stay as entered. A quantity of 1 and a unit of 6338 stays 1 × 6338. Opening the draft, or leaving Customer PN, writes the allocation for item `106683` quantity 2 when the line has none. Two of that pack allocate 4. The customer quantity is not rewritten, and the unit price is not split. A label that is itself an item allocates the customer quantity. The allocated SKU is a table row under the customer line. Allocated quantity is in the customer qty column. Unit and extension repeat the customer line in the same dollar format and are not split by the piece quantity. Find SKU sits under that row and lists the same matches as the items page as soon as it has a query. The fields use the sales-order field radius and the soft indigo focus ring. The form does not show an Allocated n of n note. An empty eBay freight line, such as Shipping, is allocated to item `4343` for the line quantity the same way. Approval of a pack uses the quantity rule in `approve_sales_order`. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025140000_approve_sales_order_optional_fields.sql` by hand. Saving the allocation does not. Deploying does not run that file. Unit price, extension, eBay fees, seller earnings, and payment amount display with a dollar sign, thousands separators, and two decimal places (`$6,338.00`). The stored values stay plain numbers. Production does not do this until this change is deployed.

Unit price, extension, order total, internal notes, and the eBay money columns are removed on the server when `can_view_financials` is false. Those columns are `ebay_transaction_fee`, `ebay_label_fee`, `ebay_ad_fee`, and `ebay_order_earnings`, all nullable. Admin, staff, and read-only can see prices. Other roles see parts and quantities. Files can contain prices, so those roles receive no attachments. Admin and staff can edit. Read-only cannot.

`payment_method` is a foreign key to `so_payment_methods`: `wire_ach`, `ebay`, `credit_card`, `check`, `paypal`, and `other`. `payment_date` and `payment_ref` record when and under what reference the payment arrived. `payment_amount` is the amount received, numeric(14,2), and stays on the sales order for now. Row level security is on for `so_payment_methods`, and only `service_role` can read or write it.

## History

`write_sales_order`, `approve_sales_order`, and `cancel_sales_order` each insert an `so_events` row with the operator initials and the database time. Adding or removing a file does the same. `so_events` rejects updates and deletes.

## Migration

`supabase/migrations/20261024070800_sales_orders.sql` is additive. `supabase/migrations/20261025100000_pack_allocation_qty.sql` replaces `approve_sales_order` so a pack label can be approved when the allocation is the customer quantity times the pack. That file also adds nullable `customers.merged_into_customer_id` (no rows are updated) and adds `conversion` to the inventory-movement reference check while keeping every reference type that is already allowed. `supabase/migrations/20261025140000_approve_sales_order_optional_fields.sql` replaces `approve_sales_order` again: a customer, one line, those allocations, and a sales order number are enough. Apply each by hand. Deploying does not run them.

If the move-inventory migration is applied after `supabase/migrations/20261025100000_pack_allocation_qty.sql` and its reference-type list does not include `conversion`, that later migration would drop `conversion`. Apply the pack file after that one, or add `conversion` to that list before applying it second.
