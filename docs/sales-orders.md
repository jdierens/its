# Sales orders

Phase 1 is the manual sales order: a draft, lines, SKU allocations, customer notes, a customer PO file, and approval. Fulfillment and invoice tables are created now so later phases do not add them. Capture from email and WhatsApp is not in this phase. An eBay pull can create a draft under the one eBay customer for an order dated 1 Jan 2026 or later. Jan reviews that draft and approves it. The pull does not assign `so_number` and does not approve the order. Orders dated before that stay out of the pull.

Sales orders do not share tables, counters, payment terms, or carrier checks with purchase orders. `so_counter` is separate from `po_counter`. Terms live in `so_payment_terms` (Net 7 is stored as `Net7` and sent to QuickBooks as `Net 7`). Payment In Advance is stored as `PIA` and sent to QuickBooks as `Payment In Advance`. Ship methods live in `so_ship_methods` (DHL WPX, FedEx IP, UPS Saver, and so on).

## Number

A draft has no number. `so_number` is an integer, and the order is shown as `SO` plus that integer, for example `SO27766`. `so_counter` is seeded at 27766, the next number at go-live.

Approval suggests the lowest unused number at or above the counter. The person approving can type a different number. `approve_sales_order` locks `so_counter`, rejects a number that is already on a sales order, and never moves the counter backwards. A number above the counter moves the counter past it. A number below the counter is allowed when it is free, and the counter stays where it is. Cancelling keeps a number that was already issued.

eBay orders use this same series. `ebay_order_id` is unique when set, and `ebay_sales_record_ref` stores the eBay sales record reference. A draft created from eBay still has no number. The sales-order list and the draft show the eBay order number and the sales record number. Approval assigns the SO number from `so_counter`, the same way as any other draft.

An eBay draft's product lines store the unit price and extension after the eBay discount. Buyer shipping is a freight line. `ebay_order_earnings` is eBay's total due the seller, not the merchandise total. `ebay_label_fee` is the shipping label bought on eBay, and seller net is earnings minus that fee. The next order pull refreshes the label fee when eBay has deducted one.

Before the first eBay pull, seed `ebay_order_id` and `ebay_sales_record_ref` from the legacy log map (Order Number and Sales Record No on the row whose REF is that SO number). The map stays out of git. Admin > Integrations > eBay accepts the CSV, updates matching sales orders, and does not create orders. Pull orders stays off until that seed row exists. Inside the 2026 window, an eBay order that already matches `ebay_order_id`, or a single `ebay_sales_record_ref`, updates that sales order instead of inserting another one. The customer on a new draft is the existing eBay Customer record. Buyer name and username are stored on the order. No per-buyer customer is created.

The form does not ask for an internal reference, and nothing requires one. `sales_orders.reference` stays in the database and is not shown on the list, the draft, or the order. A save of an order that already has one keeps that value. Order reference on the form is the customer PO. When the customer is eBay Customer, that same field is labeled eBay order id and shows `ebay_order_id`. On an eBay order, Buyer follows Customer on that row, and the eBay sales record number is the last field. Neither the internal reference nor the sales record number is a list column. The SO number itself is assigned at approval.

The form keeps that field order on the zinc surface (`ds` in `docs/design-system.md`). A sticky header holds the order identity, the sales order number, and the save, approve, and cancel actions as icons. The banner under it is row 1: channel, sales rep, customer, order date, and order reference. The field is titled Sales rep and still stores two initials. Its control is only wide enough for those letters, and the order date is tightened the same way. For the eBay customer the order reference is labeled eBay order id. An eBay order also shows Buyer after Customer and the eBay sales record number at the end of the row. An eBay order with empty payment terms or Incoterms starts as Payment In Advance (`PIA`) and FOB. Those defaults are on Admin > Settings. `supabase/migrations/20261025120000_ebay_default_terms.sql` adds the term and seeds the settings. Apply it by hand. Deploying does not run it. A value already saved on the draft stays. An eBay order leaves bill-to blank. Ship-to is not copied into bill-to on the pull, on open, or on save. The form does not show Bill to on an eBay order. Approval failures stay on the form as the database sentence, such as a line that is not allocated or a sales order number that is already used. Bill-to, ship-to, payment terms, ship method, sales rep, and order date are not required to approve. Terms and shipping are the next block, in a drawer that starts open: currency, payment terms, Incoterms, ship method, and carrier account. Ship-to sits above bill-to in the narrow column. Ship-to fields are line 1, line 2, city, state, postal code, country, then attention and phone. The line table columns are customer part number, customer description, customer qty, customer unit, and cust ext. Kind and condition stay off the screen. That row is the customer PO line. Under it, each mapped internal SKU is its own row in the same columns: the SKU name, mapped quantity, mapped unit price, and mapped extension, then a trash icon. Its tooltip is Remove mapped SKU. The customer line uses the same icon, and its tooltip is Remove line. A customer note and an attachment use that icon too. Find SKU adds the first mapped SKU. When the line already has one, the field is labeled Add another SKU and appends a row. Choosing a SKU does not replace the ones already on the line. The first mapped SKU takes the customer quantity, or the piece count when the customer part is a pack label, and the customer unit price. Another SKU takes the customer quantity that is not yet mapped, and the same unit price. The extension is that quantity times that unit price. A mapped quantity can be less than the ordered quantity. Above the lines, PO total is the sum of the customer extensions and Mapped total is the sum of the mapped extensions, with a freight or fee line that has no SKU counted in both. A line is marked when its mapped quantity or mapped extension differs from the customer PO line, and the order totals are marked when they differ. Save and Approve stay available. Focusing Find SKU fills the customer part number when the field is empty, and matching items open without another click. The form does not show an Allocated n of n note. A pack label or an exact SKU is allocated when the line is empty. There is no Allocate button. An eBay order, detected the same way as the rest of the form (an eBay order id, an eBay sales record number, or channel eBay), shows eBay amounts after the lines as one card: transaction fee, label fee, ad fee, and seller earnings in a row, with buyer total, seller earnings, shipping label, and seller net in those same columns underneath. The longer explanation stays behind About these amounts. Label fee and seller earnings keep a short info note. Any other order, including a new draft that is not eBay, shows only the buyer total in that spot. Those eBay fee and earnings values stay on the order and are still saved. Then payment: payment date, payment method, payment reference, and payment amount. Below payment, one row holds customer notes, internal notes, and attachments. The file type is Customer PO, Shipping label, Proforma, Packing list, Commercial invoice, Invoice, or Other. Customer PO stays the default. PDF only, in the private so-documents bucket. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261028222000_so_shipping_label.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run it. Until that file is applied, a shipping label is rejected by the database. Below 960px the columns stack in that same order. `payment_amount` is nullable cents on the sales order. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025083100_sales_order_payment_amount.sql` before relying on that amount. Deploying does not run it. Requested ship date stays stored and on the list, and is not a field on this form.

## Channel

`source` is how the order arrived: manual, email, WhatsApp, or eBay. `channel` is a different fact, a foreign key to `so_channels`: `ebay`, `broker_reseller`, `direct`, `supermicro_ff`, `oem_end_user`, `take_out`, `project_buy`, and `other`. Row level security is on, and only `service_role` can read or write that table.

`sheet_ref` is indexed and is not unique. `legacy_journal_ref` is nullable text and is not unique. The manual form does not fill either one.

## Invoices

`so_invoices.seq` is 1, 2, 3, and so on for that sales order. The display number is generated: seq 1 is `INV` plus the sales-order number (`INV27766`), and later invoices are `INV27766-2`, `INV27766-3`. The ITS invoice is the one the customer receives. `qbo_invoice_id`, `qbo_doc_number`, and `qbo_synced_at` are on the invoice for the later QuickBooks upload. An invoice cannot be inserted until the sales order has a number.

An approved order with a number can be printed from the sales order. Invoice and order confirmation use the same page, set in Plus Jakarta Sans. The confirmation title is ORDER CONFIRMATION. The invoice title is INVOICE. The confirmation barcode and the text under it are `SO` plus the sales order number, for example `SO27773`. The invoice barcode stays `INV` plus that number, for example `INV27773`. REF is `SO` plus the number, for example `SO27773`, with no `#`. The terms page uses that same reference. The date on that row is labeled DATE. The header mark is the official DDS logo at `public/brand/dds-logo.png`. It is transparent, so the white page shows through and there is no black plate behind it. The description column prints the item description. Under that line, HTS and ECCN print when the sales-order line has them, and a blank code falls back to the mapped item. A missing code is left off. An optional line note prints under those codes. The printed company name drops a trailing QuickBooks `(C)` or `(V)`. The name stored on the customer stays as it is, including on the sales-order form. The last item line has a bottom border, and the notes and totals sit below it with a gap. The order confirmation adds a second printed page of draft terms from `lib/orderConfirmationTerms.ts`. That page names Deep Discount Servers LLC, 3730 E Dale Ave, Henderson, NV 89044 ("DDS"), and it is marked draft for review. The invoice does not include it. Replace the sections in that file when final copy arrives. The layout stays. A later packing list that uses this layout gets the same header. Print or save as PDF to send it. Someone who cannot see prices does not get the page.

## Lines

`so_lines.line_kind` is `product`, `kit`, `freight`, `fee`, `service`, `discount`, or `other`. The default is `product`. On a draft, `so_lines` is the customer PO line. A verbal order can omit the customer part number and the description when the line has a mapped SKU. `so_lines.line_note` is an optional note for that line. The form shows HTS and ECCN under the description when the line or its mapped item has them, and edits the note in a one-line field under the mapped SKU rows and Find SKU, on a draft or an approved order. That field can grow a little. The order confirmation and invoice print the note under those codes. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261027090000_so_line_note.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run it. Approval copies the note onto each mapped SKU line. The empty starter row is not saved. The internal SKU is `so_line_allocations.item_id`, and one customer line can have several of those rows, including the same SKU more than once at different unit prices. Each allocation stores its own quantity, unit price, and extension. Those amounts do not have to equal the customer line. A product line still needs at least one mapped SKU before approval. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261027041800_so_verbal_line_text.sql` by hand so a line with no customer text can be stored. Deploying does not run it. Freight, a fee, a service, a discount, a kit, and other do not need an item. Kit contents are not expanded on the draft. There is no link from a sales-order line to a purchase-order line.

Approval copies each mapped SKU into its own `so_lines` row. The quantity, unit price, and extension on that row are the mapped SKU's. The customer part number and description are copied as reference. One allocation remains on that row, with the same item and quantity, so a pick still points at `so_line_allocations`. A line with no mapping stays as it was entered. After approval, the sales-order lines are the fulfillment lines. `so_picks`, `so_shipment_lines`, and `so_package_contents` reference the allocation. `so_invoice_lines` references `so_lines`. The app does not build pick lists yet. Invoice and Sales order confirmation are the print layout on the order. The rows those steps will read are the approved lines and their allocations, which carry the same quantity and price. The customer PO number stays on the sales order. The original PO lines are not the pick or bill source. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261027013000_so_mapped_sku_lines.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run it.

## Approve

A product line needs at least one mapped SKU. The mapped quantities do not have to add up to the customer quantity, and the mapped extension does not have to equal the customer extension. The draft shows that difference and approval still goes through. A customer part that is a pack label (`106683_2PK`, `106683(x2)`, or `106683-2PK`) and is not itself an item suggests the customer quantity times the pack. `106683_2PK` with customer quantity 1 starts as 2 of item `106683`. The user can change that quantity. Several SKUs on one line are allowed, and a one-to-one line is still one allocation row until approval, which makes it one sales-order line. Approval requires a customer, at least one line, and a sales order number. Bill-to, ship-to, payment terms, ship method, carrier account, sales rep, and order date are not required. The check is in the database function. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025140000_approve_sales_order_optional_fields.sql` and then `supabase/migrations/20261027013000_so_mapped_sku_lines.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run them. The later file is the one that allows a short quantity and writes one sales-order line per mapped SKU.

Suggested SKUs are a prefix match after uppercasing and stripping spaces, `/`, and `-`. They also match when the customer part and the SKU, part number, or OEM part number contain each other after every other punctuation character is removed. A customer part shorter than 3 characters does not match. A customer part that is an exact item SKU, or a pack label whose base SKU is exactly one item, is allocated when that line is still empty: on open, and when leaving Customer PN. There is no Allocate confirmation button. Find SKU stays on the line for a different item. It searches the same fields as the items page: SKU, name, description, model, part number, OEM name, OEM part number, product URL, attribute value, manufacturer, manufacturer alias, and eBay item id. It also searches the longest word in the customer part, splitting on spaces, punctuation, and underscores, so `FOO-BAR 106683_2PK` also looks up `106683`. The same fields match when the query and the stored text contain each other after every character that is not a letter or digit is removed, so `MCX-516A-CDAT` finds a name of `CARD\NIC\E30\100G_2Q\LP-FH\MCX516A-CDAT`. A digits-only attribute, such as cache `96.0` or size `0.96`, does not match by sitting inside a longer part number. `MZ1L2960HCJR-00A07` finds the drive whose name contains it, and it does not find a CPU whose cache is `96.0`. That attribute rule waits on `supabase/migrations/20261026004000_item_alnum_attribute_reverse.sql`. That file is not applied. Apply it by hand in the SQL editor after the punctuation-insensitive search migration. Until then, a digits-only attribute can still match inside a longer query. The extra match itself waits on `supabase/migrations/20261025230000_item_alnum_search.sql`. Until that file is applied, Find keeps the current contains match. Focusing the field fills the customer part number when it is empty, and the matches open on their own. Choosing one adds a mapped SKU. An exact match on an empty line still fills that line only. A pack that is not itself an item still starts at the piece quantity.

## Addresses and money

Ship-to is shown above bill-to. Choosing a customer copies both addresses, and they can be edited. Two icon buttons sit between ship-to and bill-to. **Copy bill to ship to** copies the bill-to street, city, state, postal code, and country onto ship-to and leaves attention and phone. **Copy ship to bill to** copies that same block the other way and leaves attention and phone on bill-to. A blank source does not replace the other address, and the form says which side is empty. The icons are hidden when Bill to is hidden, including on an eBay order, and when the order cannot be edited. An eBay order does not show Bill to, and saving it stores a blank bill-to. A draft and an approved order can both be edited, including lines, allocations, and prices. Save on an approved order keeps it approved and keeps the sales order number. Approve stays on a draft. Cancelled and closed orders stay locked, and so does every later status such as shipped. Ship-to stays on the order. Ship-to asks for attention and phone after the street, city, state, postal code, and country. **Save address to customer** writes them back with `updateContact`. A customer that already has a QuickBooks id is marked `pending`, and the existing customer push sends the change. If ship-to line 2 is blank, attention is copied into line 2. The ship-to phone updates the customer phone.

Choosing a customer also fills customer PO, channel, order date, requested ship date, terms, Incoterms, ship method, and carrier account from that customer's latest sales order. It does not copy an internal reference.

The list defaults to drafts that still need processing. Admin and staff create one from the plus at the top. Its tooltip is New sales order. Beside it, admin sees Pull eBay orders, the same pull as Admin > Integrations > eBay. Admin and staff see Check email orders. That posts to Winston's on-demand inbox routine (`on-demand-inbox-po-pass`) with `WINSTON_INBOX_WEBHOOK_URL` and `WINSTON_INBOX_WEBHOOK_KEY` on the server. The key is sent as `Authorization: Bearer` and never reaches the browser. A missing key or URL is an error on the list. HTTP 200 means Winston started the pass. The pass creates drafts only. **All orders** shows every status. This browser keeps that choice in localStorage, so leaving the list and opening Sales orders again returns to the same switch. A choice with no saved view still opens drafts. Rows are newest order date first, and an order with no order date sorts last. The same date sorts by the time the row was created, newest first. Columns are number, customer, order reference, status, order date, ship date, and total. Order reference is the eBay order id, or the customer PO when the order is not from eBay. A draft with no SO number is labeled Draft.

On a customer PO line, quantity, unit price, and extension stay consistent. Changing the unit price recomputes the extension. Changing the extension, or the quantity, recomputes the unit price and keeps the extension. On a mapped SKU, changing the quantity or the unit price recomputes the extension. Changing the extension recomputes the unit price. A quantity of 0 does not divide. Unit price is four decimals. Extension is cents. The extension is editable. `write_sales_order` stores each extension as the cent rounding of that row's quantity times its unit price. The customer line and the mapped SKU are stored separately until approval.

A customer part such as `106683_2PK` or `106683(x2)` is ITS SKU `106683` when that SKU is one item. The customer quantity, unit price, and extension stay as entered. A quantity of 1 and a unit of 6338 stays 1 × 6338 on the customer row. Opening the draft, or leaving Customer PN, writes the allocation for item `106683` quantity 2 when the line has none. Two of that pack allocate 4. The customer quantity is not rewritten. The mapped unit price starts as the customer unit price, so the mapped extension is the piece quantity times that price until the user changes the unit price. The line is marked when those amounts differ. A label that is itself an item allocates the customer quantity. The mapped SKU is a table row under the customer line, with its own quantity, unit price, and extension. Find SKU sits under that row and lists the same matches as the items page as soon as it has a query. The fields use the sales-order field radius and the soft indigo focus ring. The form does not show an Allocated n of n note. An empty eBay freight line, such as Shipping, is allocated to item `4343` for the line quantity the same way. Approval of a pack uses the quantity rule in `approve_sales_order`. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025140000_approve_sales_order_optional_fields.sql` by hand. Saving the allocation does not. Deploying does not run that file. Unit price, extension, eBay fees, seller earnings, and payment amount display with a dollar sign, thousands separators, and two decimal places (`$6,338.00`). The stored values stay plain numbers. Production does not do this until this change is deployed.

Unit price, extension, order total, internal notes, and the eBay money columns are removed on the server when `can_view_financials` is false. Those columns are `ebay_transaction_fee`, `ebay_label_fee`, `ebay_ad_fee`, and `ebay_order_earnings`, all nullable. Admin, staff, and read-only can see prices. Other roles see parts and quantities. Files can contain prices, so those roles receive no attachments. Admin and staff can edit. Read-only cannot.

`payment_method` is a foreign key to `so_payment_methods`: `wire_ach`, `ebay`, `credit_card`, `check`, `paypal`, and `other`. `payment_date` and `payment_ref` record when and under what reference the payment arrived. `payment_amount` is the amount received, numeric(14,2), and stays on the sales order for now. Row level security is on for `so_payment_methods`, and only `service_role` can read or write it.

## History

`write_sales_order`, `approve_sales_order`, and `cancel_sales_order` each insert an `so_events` row with the operator initials and the database time. Adding or removing a file does the same. `so_events` rejects updates and deletes.

## Replacement SKU

`so_line_allocations.item_id` stays the planned SKU. `actual_item_id` is the SKU that ships when it is different, the same planned and actual split `so_line_components` uses for a kit. A kit component swap stays on that component row. This column is for the product line itself: a different grade (`106683` fulfilled with `107021`), a generic planned SKU filled by a physical product, or a functional replacement such as one memory module for another. The fulfilled item has to be a non-generic product. A reason is stored on `swap_reason`, with `swapped_at` and `swapped_by`. Clearing the replacement leaves the planned SKU. Approval copies both onto the sales-order line. Picks, bin quantity, and stock units move the fulfilled SKU.

The sales-order form shows the fulfilled SKU on the mapped row and the planned SKU under it. Plus is Replace SKU. The trash icon whose tooltip is Clear replacement removes it. That choice is saved with the order and does not wait on the pick setting.

`app_settings.allow_replacement_sku_during_pick` is `false` until an admin turns on Allow replacement SKU during pick. While it is off, the pick sheet does not offer a different SKU and `set_so_allocation_replacement` with context `pick` rejects one. A replacement already saved at approval still ships, and the sheet shows planned and fulfilled. A line that already has a pick has to be undone before the SKU changes. The change writes `so_events` `sku_replaced`.

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261028073000_so_sku_replacement.sql` by hand after the stock-unit and kit migrations. Deploying does not run it.

## Picking

Warehouse → Picking is written up in [Picking](picking.md). Product and kit stock lines are the pick rows. Freight, fees, services, and service item 4343 are left off the page entirely. The item row is followed by the bin titles, then the bins. Picked lines up with pick qty. Pending lines up with the Pick button. Print ticket prints that sheet.

## Packing

Warehouse → Packing is the step after a finished pick. [Packing](packing.md) covers the packaging list, shipping bins, weights, and the rule that finishes the order. The sales order moves from `picking` to `packing` when packing is opened, and to `packed` when every picked quantity is in a bin and every bin has a weight. Freight, fees, services, and unpicked lines stay off that sheet. `packed_at` is the hook for shipping. Carrier labels and rate shopping are not built. Warehouse → Shipping lists packed orders and stops there.

A draft, an approved order, and an order being picked stay editable. Packing and packed stay locked, the same way cancelled and closed do. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261028233000_sales_order_packing.sql` by hand. Deploying does not run it.
